# Pricing — Client-Facing

Pricing for delivering the WhatsApp Auto-Reply Bot platform to a single end-customer (business). All prices exclude 14% VAT (EG) or 15% VAT (SA).

## Core Tiers

### 1. Self-Hosted Lite
**EGP 15,000** • **SAR 3,000**

What's included:
- Production-ready installation on your VPS (Ubuntu 22.04+)
- Source code archive (zipped) — full ownership
- 1× one-hour Zoom training for the admin
- 30-day bug-fix warranty (response within 48h)
- Email-only support during warranty

What's NOT included:
- Stripe account setup (we'll guide; you create)
- Domain or VPS purchase
- WhatsApp Business API onboarding (Cloud API tier)
- Custom feature development

**Best for**: A technical owner who wants ownership and can manage updates themselves.

## What Affects Final Quote

We may adjust the final price up or down based on:
- Number of rules to migrate (>50 → custom quote)
- Complexity of working-hours/holidays calendar
- Existing Google Sheets schema (if non-standard)
- Required language additions beyond AR/EN
- Compliance requirements (hospital-grade audit logging, etc.)

Quote turnaround: **48 hours** from scoping call.
